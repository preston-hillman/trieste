TRIESTE'S BLURBS

About Trieste: Three people in a room in Seattle between 2006 and 2008, recorded onto an Edirol R-09 that was left running. 130 of those recordings survive, plus four show flyers, ten photographs and a demo sleeve. The band's last show is among them - the Comet Tavern, February 17, 2008, taped from the room. No studio recording of any song has ever been found. The band is named after a bathyscaphe that went to the bottom of the Mariana Trench in 1960.

Who I'd like to meet: Already met them - Arbitron, Beestings, Coal Mine Fires, Neo Tundra Cowboy, Paul Rucker, Shipwreck, Song Sparrow Research, Twilight Zionists. See the shows below.
